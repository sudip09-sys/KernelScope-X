CHALLENGE / 01

THE MISSING PAGE

Why does logical address 5000 fail, although the numeric address looks valid?

5000 ÷ 1024 gives page 4 with offset 904. There is no valid page-table entry for page 4, so the memory-management hardware cannot translate it. The OS may raise a page fault or access violation depending on whether the page could be made present and what memory was accessed.
CHALLENGE / 02

THE 959 KB PARADOX

First Fit leaves 959 KB free after P1–P3. Why can't it place a 426 KB process?

Its remaining contiguous holes are 100, 176, 200, 300 and 183 KB. Their total is 959 KB, but the largest single hole is just 300 KB. P4 needs one contiguous block of at least 426 KB: an example of external fragmentation.
CHALLENGE / 03

WHY CACHE TRANSLATIONS?

How can address translation stay fast without consulting the page table on every memory reference?

The processor uses a Translation Lookaside Buffer (TLB) to cache recent virtual-page to physical-frame mappings. A hit avoids much of the cost of a page-table walk. Hardware and operating systems use more sophisticated TLB policies than the simplified LRU model in this lab.
